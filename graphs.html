Strongly regular graphs · constructions

New graphs for nine open parameter sets

For nine parameter sets marked “?” in Brouwer's table we give explicit strongly regular graphs: 262 graphs in all, pairwise non-isomorphic within each set. All nine sets are now marked as realised in the table, on the basis of this work.

Also in data/: the verifier verify_srg.py, the README.txt of the ancillary files and SHA256SUMS with the full hashes. SHA256SUMS also lists programs and connection sets that belong to the full archive but are not copied here.

python3 verify_srg.py srg324_136_58_56.g6 324 136 58 56

The verifier is plain Python 3 with no packages. It decodes each line and checks A² = kI + λA + μ(J − I − A) directly: every vertex has degree k, and every pair of distinct vertices has λ (adjacent) or μ (non-adjacent) common neighbours. It passes on all 264 lines of the eleven files. The two sum-graph files contain the graphs obtained from a known abelian (320,88,24) difference set of Davis and Jedwab (1997); they are not vertex-transitive (64 automorphisms) and are not counted among the new graphs.

Independent review was uneven, and the paper says exactly what it covered. The first drafts (graphs reported on 30 September) were refereed by separate Claude agents with their own code. Several later additions (from 1 and 2 October) were not refereed independently at the time. On 6 October a Codex referee re-verified strong regularity and pairwise non-isomorphism of all 256 graphs then listed, with its own program, without recomputing every invariant. The eight srg(324,114,36,42) nos. 15–22, added on 7 October 2026, have not been refereed independently.
