Eight strongly regular graphs with parameters (243, 22, 1, 2)

In an srg(243,22,1,2) every edge lies in exactly one triangle and two non-adjacent vertices have exactly two common neighbours. For about fifty years the Berlekamp–van Lint–Seidel graph of 1973 was the only known example. In October 2026 D. Djematene found seven more: three in his Zenodo deposit, and four in a public repository dated 13 October 2026. Three of those four (called Γ₅, Γ₆, Γ₇ below) we found again on 17 and 18 October, independently, by lifting orbit matrices under subgroups, without knowing of the repository; the fourth, with automorphism group of order 162, we did not find. The eight are pairwise non-isomorphic. The classification of the parameter set is open.

Correction, 5 November 2026. Until today this page, and our note of 20 October, presented Γ₅, Γ₆ and Γ₇ as new graphs of ours. They are Djematene's: his files predate ours by four days. We compared canonical forms of his eight graphs with ours on 5 November 2026. What remains ours is an independent confirmation of the three graphs and the description below of how they arise as lifts.

This is a different parameter set from the unique srg(243,110,37,60).

8 graphs known Γ₅, Γ₆, Γ₇: our note of 20 Oct 2026 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23491895) claims them as new; correction pending Γ₆, Γ₇: verified by two independent checks; referee pass completed 18 Oct 2026 (ACCEPT) computer-assisted Brouwer's table: row “+”, not unique

The grid counts of the first seven are pairwise distinct, and the eighth graph is the only one with |Aut| = 162, so the eight graphs are pairwise non-isomorphic; canonical forms computed with nauty on 5 November 2026 confirm this. In Djematene's own numbering our Γ₅, Γ₆, Γ₇ are his fifth, sixth and eighth graphs, and the graph in the last row is his seventh. The group order and the orbits in the last row are our computation on his file. The 3-rank alone does not separate them: Γ₇ and Γ₃ both have 90. The values for Djematene's graphs were computed from his deposit and agree with his table. Γ₆ and Γ₇ have four vertex orbits and |Aut| = 108, which is not divisible by 243, so they are neither vertex-transitive nor Cayley graphs; the same holds for the eighth graph. The three graphs of Djematene's deposit (Γ′, Γ₃, Γ₄) are 3-fold covers of the Brouwer–Haemers graph srg(81,20,1,6) along triangle spreads, in his terminology. Γ₅ is not.

Γ₅

A Cayley graph on SmallGroup(243, 51)

Let R = C₃ × (C₃ ≀ C₃) ≅ SmallGroup(243,51), written as triples (a; x; t) with a, t ∈ ℤ₃ and x = (x₀, x₁, x₂) ∈ ℤ₃³, with product (a; x; t)(b; y; u) = (a + b; x + y(t); t + u), where y(t)i = yi−t. Γ₅ is the Cayley graph on R with g ~ h if and only if hg⁻¹ ∈ S, where S is the 22-set below. Each word is read as a, x₀x₁x₂, t.

00022 00100 00120 00200 00210 01001 01200 02100 02112 02121 10112 11010 11121 12001 12020 12022 20012 21010 21101 22020 22021 22122

S is inverse-closed and does not contain the identity. The automorphism group has order 2916 = 2²·3⁶ and is vertex-transitive with three orbits on edges, so Γ₅ is not edge-transitive. All nine regular subgroups of Aut Γ₅ are non-abelian, so Γ₅ is not a Cayley graph of an abelian group and not a translation graph. Γ₅ has no triangle spread whose triangles are pairwise joined by 0 or 3 edges. Hence it is not a 3-fold cover of the Brouwer–Haemers graph along a spread, and Djematene's re-voltaging construction cannot produce it.

How we found it again. Djematene's repository gives Γ₅ by explicit connection sets on three copies of ℤ₃⁴ and notes that it is a Cayley graph on nine non-abelian regular subgroups. Our route was different. Take Djematene's Γ₄ and the unique class of subgroups H ≅ SmallGroup(486,185) of Aut Γ₄ with three vertex orbits of size 81. Every H-invariant graph on the same vertices is a union of the 90 orbits of H on pairs. We enumerated all such graphs with the same orbit matrix that are strongly regular. Three encodings and two SAT solvers give the same 243 solutions in three classes: Γ₄, the BvLS graph and Γ₅.

Computationally, Γ₅ has no coclique meeting the Hoffman bound 45 (two independent SAT runs, no proof certificate). This is stated as an observation only.

Γ₆ and Γ₇

Lifts under subgroups of order 108

The same method was applied to Djematene's Γ₃ (|Aut| = 864) and two subgroups H of order 108, each with four vertex orbits. The models have 302 and 328 Boolean variables, one per orbit of H on pairs. Among the solutions are two classes not isomorphic to any of the five graphs above: Γ₆, from the first subgroup, and Γ₇, from the second. Both are in Djematene's repository of 13 October, where they are obtained from Γ₅ by changing the edges inside a set of 162 vertices. Both have full automorphism group of order 108, with vertex orbits 27 + 54 + 54 + 108.

Two independent checks confirmed them. The first is exact A² and a standalone verifier. The second is a separate verification session that used its own code and two canonicalisers, dreadnaut and pynauty. That session also compared the graphs with every stored srg(243,22,1,2) and computed the invariants of the table, which were controlled on the five earlier graphs. A referee pass was completed on 18 Oct 2026 (verdict ACCEPT); it checked the graphs, not their novelty. The note was deposited on Zenodo on 20 October 2026, doi:10.5281/zenodo.23491895.
