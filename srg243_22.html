Seven strongly regular graphs with parameters (243, 22, 1, 2)

In an srg(243,22,1,2) every edge lies in exactly one triangle and two non-adjacent vertices have exactly two common neighbours. For about fifty years the Berlekamp–van Lint–Seidel graph of 1973 was the only known example. In October 2026 Djematene deposited three more. We add three: a vertex-transitive Cayley graph Γ₅ of a non-abelian group of order 243, and two graphs Γ₆ and Γ₇ with automorphism group of order 108. The seven are pairwise non-isomorphic. Their invariants alone show this, without a canonical labelling. The classification of the parameter set is open. This is a different parameter set from the unique srg(243,110,37,60).

The grid counts are pairwise distinct, so the seven graphs are pairwise non-isomorphic without a canonical labelling. The 3-rank alone does not separate them: Γ₇ and Γ₃ both have 90. The values for Djematene's graphs were computed from his deposit and agree with his table. Γ₆ and Γ₇ have four vertex orbits and |Aut| = 108, which is not divisible by 243, so they are neither vertex-transitive nor Cayley graphs. Djematene's three graphs are 3-fold covers of the Brouwer–Haemers graph srg(81,20,1,6) along triangle spreads, in his terminology. Γ₅ is not.

Γ₅

A Cayley graph on SmallGroup(243, 51)

Let R = C₃ × (C₃ ≀ C₃) ≅ SmallGroup(243,51), written as triples (a; x; t) with a, t ∈ ℤ₃ and x = (x₀, x₁, x₂) ∈ ℤ₃³, with product (a; x; t)(b; y; u) = (a + b; x + y(t); t + u), where y(t)i = yi−t. Γ₅ is the Cayley graph on R with g ~ h if and only if hg⁻¹ ∈ S, where S is the 22-set below. Each word is read as a, x₀x₁x₂, t.

S is inverse-closed and does not contain the identity. The automorphism group has order 2916 = 2²·3⁶ and is vertex-transitive with three orbits on edges, so Γ₅ is not edge-transitive. All nine regular subgroups of Aut Γ₅ are non-abelian, so Γ₅ is not a Cayley graph of an abelian group and not a translation graph. Γ₅ has no triangle spread whose triangles are pairwise joined by 0 or 3 edges. Hence it is not a 3-fold cover of the Brouwer–Haemers graph along a spread, and Djematene's re-voltaging construction cannot produce it.

How it was found. Take Djematene's Γ₄ and the unique class of subgroups H ≅ SmallGroup(486,185) of Aut Γ₄ with three vertex orbits of size 81. Every H-invariant graph on the same vertices is a union of the 90 orbits of H on pairs. We enumerated all such graphs with the same orbit matrix that are strongly regular. Three encodings and two SAT solvers give the same 243 solutions in three classes: Γ₄, the BvLS graph and Γ₅.

Computationally, Γ₅ has no coclique meeting the Hoffman bound 45 (two independent SAT runs, no proof certificate). This is stated as an observation only.

Γ₆ and Γ₇

Lifts under subgroups of order 108

The same method was applied to Djematene's Γ₃ (|Aut| = 864) and two subgroups H of order 108, each with four vertex orbits. The models have 302 and 328 Boolean variables, one per orbit of H on pairs. Among the solutions are two classes not isomorphic to any known graph: Γ₆, from the first subgroup, and Γ₇, from the second. Both have full automorphism group of order 108, with vertex orbits 27 + 54 + 54 + 108.

Two independent checks confirmed them. The first is exact A² and a standalone verifier. The second is a separate verification session that used its own code and two canonicalisers, dreadnaut and pynauty. That session also compared the graphs with every stored srg(243,22,1,2) and computed the invariants of the table, which were controlled on the five earlier graphs. A referee pass was completed on 18 Oct 2026 (verdict ACCEPT). The note is not yet deposited; the DOI will follow.
