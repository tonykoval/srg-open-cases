Strongly regular graphs · open case from Brouwer's table

There is no strongly regular graph with parameters (99, 42, 21, 15)

No graph on 99 vertices, each of degree 42, where adjacent vertices share 21 neighbours and non-adjacent vertices share 15. (This is not Conway's 99-graph problem, srg(99, 14, 1, 2).) The proof turns the graph into lattice vectors, narrows the possible lattices to a single one with two embeddings, and then searches every candidate vector exhaustively. No symmetry of the graph is assumed.

Step 1 · spectrum

From eigenvalues to vectors

The adjacency matrix A has three eigenvalues. The matrix G = 3I + A + J sends the eigenvalue −3 to 0, so G is positive semidefinite of rank 22 and is the Gram matrix of 99 vectors in 22 dimensions.

eigenvalue of A42multiplicity 1  → G: 144
eigenvalue of A9multiplicity 21  → G: 12
eigenvalue of A−3multiplicity 77  → G: 0

Each vertex becomes a vector of norm 4; adjacent vertices have inner product 2, non-adjacent ones 1. They span an even lattice L of rank 22. For a maximal even overlattice N, the vector y = s/36 (s the sum of the vertices) has norm 11 and lies in the dual N# but not in N, while 2y ∈ N.

Controls

The same machinery on graphs that exist

Two known graphs have the same mechanism: smallest eigenvalue −3, and y₀ of order 2 with q(y₀) = 1. Both have odd rank, so their 2-part is ℤ/4 rather than ℤ/4 × ℤ/2. Among feasible parameter sets with n ≤ 1300, (99, 42, 21, 15) is the only one of the exact target shape, so no control of that shape exists in this range.

Verification

How each step was checked

The paper's programs form groups that share no code: the first implementation, the genus audit, the second implementation and the third implementation written for the review. On 6 October 2026 a further, separate Claude session re-implemented the weakest steps from the paper text and Borcherds' data alone, without PARI.

What remains weaker. The lattice builder of the paper has one implementation, mitigated by the mass identities and independent witness checks. Orbits and dual vectors use PARI in all three of the paper's implementations; a missed dual vector would only weaken the constraints. The standalone checker cannot confirm that the survivor lists are complete; that rests on the three enumerators and their count certificates. The controls do not exercise the exact ℤ/4 × ℤ/2 shape. The 6 October check did not compute |Aut| for all 665 lattices or the mass of 𝒢₈ independently. It found no discrepancy.

The derivations, code, audits, review and drafting were carried out with extensive help from AI agents (Claude); ideas from gpt-6-astra and JetBrains' Junie are credited in the paper. No step rests on the judgement of an AI system: every claim is proved in the text or reduced to a named computation that can be rerun. The paper has not yet been reviewed by a human referee.
