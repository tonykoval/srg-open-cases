A strongly regular graph with parameters (217, 66, 15, 22)

On 18 October 2026 Brouwer's table marked srg(217,66,15,22) and its complement srg(217,150,105,100) with “?”: no graph was known. The row's comment is “pg(6,10,2)?”, because these are the parameters of the point graph of a hypothetical partial geometry pg(6,10,2). We construct one graph Γ, and hence its complement. Γ lives on ℤ₃₁ × {0, …, 6}. It is a 7 × 7 block-circulant graph whose connection sets are unions of {0} and cosets of the subgroup of index 6 of 𝔽₃₁*. Strong regularity follows from the cyclotomic numbers of order 6 over 𝔽₃₁. Γ is not geometric, so the existence of pg(6,10,2) remains open.

A pg(s,t,α) with s = 6, t = 10, α = 2 has v = (s+1)(st+α)/α = 217 points, k = s(t+1) = 66, λ = s − 1 + t(α − 1) = 15 and μ = α(t+1) = 22. No reference is given in the row, and we found no mention of the parameter set in the literature.

Construction

Seven blocks over ℤ₃₁

3 is a primitive root modulo 31, and the subgroup of index 6 of 𝔽₃₁* is C = ⟨2⟩ = {1, 2, 4, 8, 16}. Write Cr = rC. The six cosets are C₁, C₃, C₅, C₇, C₁₁ and C₁₅, with −C₁ = C₁₅, −C₃ = C₇ and −C₅ = C₁₁. Put Z = {0}, and write + for disjoint union. The vertices are (x, j) with x ∈ ℤ₃₁ and block j ∈ {0, …, 6}. Two vertices (x, i) and (y, j) are adjacent if and only if y − x ∈ Sij, where (Definition 2.1 of the paper)

S₀₁ = Z + C₅ + C₁₅S₀₂ = Z + C₇ + C₁₁S₀₃ = Z + C₁ + C₃
S₁₁ = C₁ + C₁₅S₁₂ = Z + C₇S₁₃ = Z + C₁₁
S₁₄ = Z + C₃ + C₇S₁₅ = Z + C₃ + C₅S₁₆ = Z + C₅ + C₁₅
S₂₂ = C₅ + C₁₁S₂₃ = Z + C₁S₂₄ = Z + C₇ + C₁₁
S₂₅ = Z + C₁ + C₁₅S₂₆ = Z + C₇ + C₁₅
S₃₃ = C₃ + C₇S₃₄ = Z + C₁ + C₁₁S₃₅ = Z + C₁ + C₃
S₃₆ = Z + C₅ + C₁₁

completed by the rules S₀₀ = ∅, S0,j+3 = S0j, Si+3,j+3 = Sij (1 ≤ i ≤ j ≤ 3) and Sji = −Sij. The diagonal sets are symmetric and do not contain 0, so Γ is a simple undirected graph. Every set is invariant under multiplication by 2. The partition into blocks is equitable: a vertex of block 0 has 11 neighbours in each other block, and the other entries of the 7 × 7 quotient are 10, 6 and 11, every row summing to 66.

Proof idea

Cyclotomic numbers of order 6, and 49 identities reduced to 6

Strong regularity is the matrix identity A² = 44I − 7A + 22J. The matrix A is a 7 × 7 array of 31 × 31 circulants, and circulants multiply like elements of the group ring ℤ[ℤ₃₁]. Every Sij lies in the rank-7 subring spanned by 1 and the six Gaussian periods η₀, …, η₅. Products of periods are given by the cyclotomic numbers (i, j)₆ of 𝔽₃₁, a 6 × 6 table printed in the paper that can be checked by hand from the powers of 3 modulo 31. The matrix identity becomes 49 block identities in this ring.

The automorphisms (x, j) ↦ (7x, πj) with π = (1 3 2)(4 6 5) and (x, j) ↦ (x, τj) with τ = (1 4)(2 5)(3 6), together with Sji = −Sij, reduce the 49 identities to six block pairs: (0,0), (0,1), (1,1), (1,2), (1,4) and (1,5). The paper works the block (0,0) in full. A standard-library program evaluates all 49 from the cyclotomic table alone and checks the symmetry claims.

Remarks in the paper

The block shape, and uniqueness within the family

Other primes. Take a prime p ≡ 1 mod 6 and a graph on ℤp × {0, …, 6} of the same shape: block 0 a coclique, the Kx cliques. It can be strongly regular only if p + 14 divides 1260. The only such primes are p = 7, giving (49,18,7,6), the parameters of the Latin square graphs LS₃(7), and p = 31, giving (217,66,15,22). The same class table transferred to p = 7, 13, 19, 37 and 43 gives regular graphs that are not strongly regular.

Within the ℤ₃₁ cyclotomic family. If an srg(217,66,15,22) has a semiregular automorphism of order 31, its quotient is one of exactly 15 matrices, and 4 of them pass the eigenvalue-multiplicity test. A complete search finds Γ as the only graph on ℤ₃₁ × {0, …, 6} whose connection sets are unions of {0} and orbits of a multiplier group of index 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 or 10. For index 15 and 30 ({±1} and the trivial group) the search was not completed, and nothing is claimed there. In particular no srg(217,66,15,22) has a semiregular automorphism of order 31 whose seven orbits are all cocliques. The classification of the parameter set is open.

How it was found

A search with prescribed intransitive groups

The graph came from a search for strongly regular graphs on the open rows with v ≤ 300 that admit a prescribed intransitive group with 3 to 12 orbits. CP-SAT enumerates the quotient matrices of the orbit partition, and a SAT model over the orbits of the group on pairs lifts each quotient. This extends the method of the two-orbit search that gave the graphs on 160, 232, 261 and 280 vertices. The search ran on three servers on disjoint shards and found the same graph twice, independently. One server found it under the Frobenius group C₃₁:C₁₅, with orbits 31 + 93 + 93. Another found it under C₂ × (C₃₁:C₅), with orbits 31 + 62 + 62 + 62. Both groups are subgroups of Aut Γ.

A separate verification session with its own code checked A² exactly, the spectrum, the group with two canonicalisers, the cliques and further invariants. It also read off the cyclotomic description from the group, and the graph built from that description is isomorphic to the search graph. A referee session re-derived the hand proofs and reproduced the computations; its minor corrections are in version 1.0. Strong regularity is confirmed three ways: the cyclotomic proof, a direct common-neighbour count from the definition, and the standalone verifier on the graph6 file.
