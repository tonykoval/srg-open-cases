Strongly regular graphs · uniqueness without a group assumption

The strongly regular graphs with parameters (243, 110, 37, 60) and (253, 112, 36, 60) are unique

The Delsarte graph is the only srg(243,110,37,60), and the M₂₃ graph on the 253 heptads of S(4,7,23) is the only srg(253,112,36,60); so the complementary parameter sets are realised uniquely too. No symmetry of the graph is assumed. Each graph is a system of equiangular lines at angle arccos 1/5 in ℝ²³; lattice arguments force the lines into the 276-line system of a norm-6 vector of the Leech lattice, where an exhaustive enumeration finds only the known graph. The method is that of the two-graph paper.

The theorem

Two graphs, each the only one with its parameters

srg(243, 110, 37, 60)the Delsarte graph · |Aut| = |3⁵:(2 × M₁₁)| = 3,849,120Let C be the ternary Golay code of length 11 and C⊥ its dual, a [11,5,6] code with weights 0, 6, 9. The Cayley graph on the 243 words of C⊥, two words adjacent when their difference has weight 9, is the Delsarte graph. Every srg(243,110,37,60) is isomorphic to it.
srg(253, 112, 36, 60)the M₂₃ graph · |Aut| = |M₂₃| = 10,200,960The 253 heptads of the Steiner system S(4,7,23) meet in 1 or 3 points; the graph on the heptads with adjacency “meet in one point” is the M₂₃ graph. Every srg(253,112,36,60) is isomorphic to it.

In the language of lines: a set of 243 (resp. 253) equiangular lines in ℝ²³ at angle arccos 1/5 whose Seidel matrix switches to that of the complementary graph is isometric to the complement, in the 276-line system of Goethals and Seidel, of 33 lines whose Seidel matrix switches to J − I − 2(11K₃) (resp. of 23 lines whose Seidel matrix switches to J − I).

Uniqueness among rank-3 graphs is a statement about graphs with a given group. A web search on 10–11 October 2026 found no uniqueness proof for either parameter set and no classification of subsystems of the 276 lines of size 243 or 253.

Step 1 · spectrum

From the complement to equiangular lines

The proof works with the complements srg(243,132,81,60) and srg(253,140,87,65), for which the smallest eigenvalue is s = −3 and the other nontrivial eigenvalue r has multiplicity f = 22. The Seidel matrix S′ = 2A − J + I then has only one negative eigenvalue, −5, so G = (5I + S′)/2 is positive semidefinite of rank 23.

vA (complement)S′ = 2A − J + IG = (5I + S′)/2

253140¹ 25²² (−3)²³⁰28¹ 51²² (−5)²³⁰33/2 on 𝟏, 28 (22×), 0

So G is the Gram matrix of vectors wᵢ ∈ ℝ²³ with wᵢ² = 5/2 and ⟨wᵢ, wⱼ⟩ = ±1/2: v equiangular lines at angle arccos 1/5. The graph is determined by the line system, because the eigenvalue on 𝟏 is simple. The vectors wᵢ ± wⱼ generate an even lattice K of rank 23; all wᵢ lie in K# and in one class γ of order 2 with q(γ) = 1/2.

The counts are what one expects if Co₃, the stabiliser of b, is transitive on each family with stabiliser the automorphism group of the graph. The referee also derived both counts by hand from the McLaughlin graph srg(275,112,30,56): 276 · 4,050 / 23 = 48,600 from its 22-cocliques, and 276 · 15,400 / 33 = 128,800 from its 15,400 edges. None of these identities is used in the proof, which is group-free on purpose.

The derivation was done by Claude Opus. Each of the two blind audits, one per parameter set, was done in a separate session by Claude Fable, a different Claude model of the same vendor, instructed to be adversarial; each re-derived every step by hand or with new code before opening any of the derivation's files. Both returned the verdict sound, with no error and no gap; their two presentation points (the 12 vs. 16 genera, and the parity of s for half-integral z) are fixed in the paper. A further separate referee session (Claude Fable) checked the logical chain line by line, reran the checker (“ALL CHECKS PASSED (169 checks)”) and the O₂₆ count, counted |T(r)| by brute force for 3000 random r, recomputed the 368 rank-25 lattices and the rootless rank-26 entry, and recommended minor revision with no mathematical error and no gap; its points are applied. The independence is of session and of code, not of vendor. The canonical forms written independently by the derivation and the audits are byte-identical. The grade is computer-assisted: the two Leech enumerations cannot be done by hand, and isomorphism rests on nauty.
