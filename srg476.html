Strongly regular graphs · open case from Brouwer's table

There is no strongly regular graph with parameters (476, 133, 60, 28)

No graph on 476 vertices, each of degree 133, where adjacent vertices share 60 neighbours and non-adjacent vertices share 28; hence none with the complementary parameters (476,342,236,270) either. The vertices become vectors of an even lattice of rank 35. By hand, its maximal even overlattice has one of exactly two discriminant forms, and an elementary counting lemma bounds its roots. Gluing into even unimodular lattices of rank 40 turns harmonic theta series into linear equations, and two exact Farkas certificates show that, together with the lemma, they have no solution. No symmetry of the graph is assumed.

Step 1 · spectrum

From eigenvalues to vectors

The restricted eigenvalues are the roots of x² − 32x − 105 = (x − 35)(x + 3). The matrix G = A + 3I + J sends the eigenvalue −3 to 0, so G is positive semidefinite of rank 35 and is the Gram matrix of 476 vectors in 35 dimensions.

eigenvalue of A133multiplicity 1  → G: 612
eigenvalue of A35multiplicity 34  → G: 38
eigenvalue of A−3multiplicity 441  → G: 0

Each vertex becomes a vector of norm 4; adjacent vertices have inner product 2, non-adjacent ones 1. They span an even lattice L of rank 35, and we fix a maximal even overlattice N; its discriminant form is anisotropic. The normalized sum y = (Σ vᵢ)/204 has norm 7 and inner product 3 with every vertex; it lies in N# but not in N, and 2y ∈ N. The parameters pass the absolute bound (476 ≤ 629) and both Krein conditions (−1512 ≤ 672, 160 ≤ 168480).

Step 4 · the counting lemma, worked

Roots, and why a norm-4 vector meets at most three vertices in 3

For every x ∈ N the inequality of step 2 with a = 1 reads Σ (zᵢ − 1)(zᵢ − 2) = 38ν + 82t² − 612t + 952 ≥ 0, with t ∈ ℤ.

Every root is orthogonal to y. A root r has t² ≤ 14, so |t| ≤ 3. Since z² ≥ |z| for integers, Σ zᵢ² ≥ |Σ zᵢ|:

t = ±1: 76 + 82 = 158 ≥ 204 false t = ±2: 76 + 328 = 404 ≥ 408 false t = 3: 76 + 738 − 1836 + 952 = −70 < 0 impossible (t = −3 by negation)

The budget of a vector of S₄. For w ∈ S₄ (w² = 4, ⟨w, y⟩ = 3) the same inequality is an exact budget:

Σ (zᵢ − 1)(zᵢ − 2) = 152 + 738 − 1836 + 952 = 6

Every term is a nonnegative even integer. Each value of zᵢ costs:

By Cauchy–Schwarz |zᵢ| ≤ 4, with equality only if w = ±vᵢ. With a budget of 6, a non-vertex w has at most three entries equal to 3. The map (r, i) ↦ (vᵢ − r, i) is a bijection from the pairs with ⟨r, vᵢ⟩ = 1 onto the pairs (w, i) with w ∈ S₄ not a vertex vector and ⟨w, vᵢ⟩ = 3. Each root has 38 such pairs, so

38·|R₀| ≤ 3·(|S₄| − 476)

The step “every root is orthogonal to y” is specific to the parameters; here it holds by the computation −70 < 0. Unlike for (405,132,63,33), no closed-form identity for |S₄| was extracted; the contradiction comes from the exact certificates.

Controls for the lemma · a remark of the paper

The lemma on graphs that exist

For a graph with smallest eigenvalue −3 represented by A + 3I + J, the same counting gives (r + 3)|R₀| ≤ 3(|S₄| − v), provided no root has t = ±tv. It was evaluated on maximal even overlattices of two Latin-square graphs, from an exact census of all vectors of N# of norm ≤ 4, computed separately by the derivation and by the audit:

graphrankD(N)|R₀||S₄|(r + 3)|R₀| ≤ 3(|S₄| − v)
L₃(13) = srg(169,36,13,6)37⟨5/4⟩01690 ≤ 0: sharp
L₃(12) = srg(144,33,12,6)34⟨8/15⟩36288432 ≤ 432: sharp

In both, no root has t = ±tv; on L₃(12) every non-vertex w ∈ S₄ has exactly three entries 3. The companion paper on (405,132,63,33) lists six further graphs with smallest eigenvalue −3 on which the inequality holds.

A Farkas certificate is a rational vector y with (yᵀA)v ≥ 0 for every unknown after shifting the unknowns by their lower bounds, and y·c′ > 0; then 0 = (yᵀA)n′ + y·c′ > 0 for every n′ ≥ 0. Both certificates are VALID in the standalone checker, in the audit's and the referee's own exact code, and in a third minimal check written for the paper. Without the lemma row both systems are feasible; a floating-point LP over the lemma-free g₀ equations suggests |R₀| ≳ 985, which the paper marks as heuristic and does not use.

The audit's systems were larger than the paper's: for g₀ three lattices × four gluings, 111 unknowns, 5,808 equations of rank 75; for g₁ two lattices × four gluings, 113 unknowns, 3,872 equations of rank 80. Both are exactly infeasible with the lemma (the bound |S₄| ≥ 476 is not used, n(0,4,0) ≥ 63308 is) and exactly feasible without it. A single construction without the decider's information is feasible, so the exclusion genuinely uses the admissibility of types.

The derivation was done by Claude Opus (10 October 2026). The blind audit was done by Claude Fable (10–11 October 2026), a different Claude model, as an independent re-derivation with its own code; it read the derivation's files only in a second phase, through a restricted unpickler. An independent referee (a fresh Claude Fable session, 11 October 2026, Python standard library only, nothing imported) re-checked the hand proofs, the certificates, the rows and every type exclusion, and recommended acceptance after minor revisions, with no fatal or major finding; all its points are applied in the published version. The independence achieved is independence of session and of code, not of vendor. The grade is computer-assisted: the forms, the counting lemma, the gluing and the modular-form relations are proved in the text; the type lists and the two certificates are computer outputs. Of the 337 omitted types, 263 are rejected by the quadratic bound and can be checked by hand; the other 74 rest on the exhaustive subset-sum test, run by two separately written solver-free programs.
